Aim:
To develop the problem statement for the Online ATM System.

Problem Statement
The existing ATM system provides basic banking services but may involve limitations such as limited transaction options, security concerns, and dependency on manual verification. Customers need a convenient and secure system to perform banking transactions such as cash withdrawal, balance enquiry, cash deposit, fund transfer, and PIN change.

The Online ATM System provides a computerized platform where users can securely authenticate themselves and perform various banking transactions. The system verifies customer details, processes transactions, updates account information, and maintains transaction records.

Objectives
Reduce manual banking work.

Provide secure and convenient ATM services.

Enable online balance enquiry.

Allow cash withdrawal and deposit.

Provide fund transfer facilities.

Enable PIN change.

Maintain transaction records securely.

Reduce transaction processing time.

Modules
User/Card Authentication

User Login/PIN Verification

Balance Enquiry

Cash Withdrawal

Cash Deposit

Fund Transfer

PIN Change

Mini Statement

Admin/Bank Management
